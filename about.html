Equally at home on the largest stage and the most intimate setting, Larry uses his powerful magic to reach across the footlights and touch the audience for a very entertaining show. Larry's show features a lot of audience participation, comedy, and variety. It is the perfect entertainment for banquets, trade shows, hospitality suites, cocktail parties or business meetings. Ask Larry to incorporate your message into the show -- your audience will remember it for a long time!
